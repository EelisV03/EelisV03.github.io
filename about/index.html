About me

Curious about how machines think and move.

I am Eelis Viskari, a robotics engineering student at Tampere University building broad technical skills across intelligent machines.

Profile

From code to mechanics

A multidisciplinary engineering mindset.

Robotics is most interesting to me where software meets the physical world. I enjoy understanding how individual components—mechanisms, sensors, control and software—work together as one reliable system.

My studies and projects currently focus on ROS 2, control systems, mechanical design, programming and intelligent machines. I am continuously developing both my theoretical foundation and practical problem-solving skills.

  • Learn by buildingTurn theory into models, simulations and working prototypes.
